## 1. Executive Summary
Create a simple, digital, and measurable onboarding experience for new group insurance customers, brokers, and internal teams.

New group onboarding relies on fragmented information collection, document exchanges, manual validation, and operational handoffs. This creates avoidable delays, rework, limited status visibility, and an inconsistent sponsor experience.

## 2. Strategic Intent
The initiative links business strategy to coordinated epics, features, delivery increments, and measurable outcomes.

### Desired outcomes
- Reduce end-to-end onboarding cycle time.
- Reduce manual data entry and avoidable rework.
- Improve first-pass completeness and data quality.
- Give sponsors, brokers, and internal teams clearer status visibility.
- Provide measurable operational and customer outcomes.

## 3. Scope
### In scope
- Digital collection and validation of new-group information.
- Structured underwriting intake and referrals.
- Digital document collection and eligible e-signature workflows.
- Workflow, task, exception, and handoff management.
- Sponsor and broker collaboration.
- Operational dashboards and benefits measurement.

### Out of scope for this illustrative initiative
- Renewal and in-force contract change processes.
- Claims administration.
- Final product, pricing, legal, privacy, security, and compliance decisions.
- Replacement of systems not approved through architecture and investment governance.

## 7. Governance and Decision Gates
1. **Initiative definition:** confirm problem, desired outcomes, sponsorship, scope, and funding approach.
2. **Epic approval:** validate business value, boundaries, dependencies, and measurable outcomes.
3. **Feature readiness:** approve the Feature Canvas and confirm that major unknowns have owners.
4. **Delivery readiness:** confirm stories, acceptance criteria, dependencies, designs, controls, and test approach.
5. **Release readiness:** confirm operational, technical, training, support, and change readiness.
6. **Outcome review:** compare actual KPI results with the approved baseline and target.

## 8. Key Dependencies and Constraints
- Business process and policy decisions.
- Identity, access, data, integration, notification, document, and reporting capabilities.
- Legal, privacy, security, accessibility, records-management, and bilingual-content requirements.
- Availability of business SMEs and operational change capacity.
- Agreement on authoritative data sources and ownership.

## 9. Principal Risks
| Risk | Illustrative response |
|---|---|
| Digitizing an inefficient process | Complete current-state and future-state analysis before automating |
| Incomplete decision ownership | Establish named decision owners and escalation paths |
| Poor input data | Use preventive validation and visible correction workflows |
| Low adoption | Involve users early and plan communications, training, and support |
| Fragmented delivery | Maintain traceability from KPI to epic, feature, story, test, and release |
| Unmeasured value | Establish baselines and measurement ownership before release |
