## 1. Epic Summary
Provide reliable visibility into onboarding progress, bottlenecks, quality, and outcomes.

**Expected value:** Evidence-based management, earlier intervention, and transparent benefits realization.

## 2. Problem / Opportunity
The current onboarding journey includes manual exchanges, fragmented information, delayed validation, and limited status visibility. This epic addresses the portion of that problem described in the objective above.

## 3. Epic Hypothesis
We believe that provide reliable visibility into onboarding progress, bottlenecks, quality, and outcomes. This should contribute to evidence-based management, earlier intervention, and transparent benefits realization. The hypothesis must be tested through approved feature KPIs and initiative outcomes.

## 4. Scope
### In scope
- **Onboarding Status Dashboard:** Show portfolio and case-level status, ownership, milestones, and aging.
- **Bottleneck and Aging Analysis:** Identify where cases wait, fail validation, or require repeated follow-up.
- **Outcome and Benefits Reporting:** Track initiative KPIs and compare actual outcomes with approved targets.

### Out of scope
- Capabilities assigned to another epic in INIT-001.
- Final technical design and vendor selection.
- Unapproved policy, legal, privacy, security, or operational changes.

## 6. Epic Success Measures
- Contribution to the parent initiative's cycle-time, completeness, manual-handling, rework, satisfaction, and adoption measures.
- Feature-level KPIs are defined in each Feature Canvas.
- Baselines, targets, measurement frequency, and data owners must be approved before implementation.
