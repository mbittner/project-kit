## 1. Change Summary
Business leaders, product managers, operations managers, and business analysts move from fragmented status reporting and anecdotal bottleneck awareness to a shared onboarding status dashboard, systematic bottleneck/aging analysis, and a KPI scorecard tied to approved targets.

## 2. Business Driver
- **Problem being solved:** Limited status visibility, and lack of a reliable, shared way to see progress, bottlenecks, quality, and outcomes across onboarding.
- **Expected value:** Evidence-based management, earlier intervention, and transparent benefits realization.
- **What happens if we do nothing:** Bottlenecks and quality issues are found late, benefits from other epics are difficult to demonstrate, and status reporting remains manual and inconsistent across teams.

## 3. Impacted Stakeholder Groups
| Stakeholder group | Role today | Role after change | Impact level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business leader | Receives ad hoc, manually compiled status updates | Reviews a live portfolio/case-level dashboard | Medium |
| Product Manager | Manually assembles KPI evidence for benefits reporting | Uses a KPI scorecard with baseline/target comparison | Medium |
| Operations manager | Identifies bottlenecks reactively through escalations | Uses aging and bottleneck analysis to intervene proactively | High |
| Business Analyst | Manually reconciles data for reporting | Relies on a consistent, shared reporting source | Low |

## 4. Nature of the Change
- **Process change:** Status and benefits reporting move from manual compilation to a standing, shared dashboard and scorecard.
- **Tool/system change:** Introduction of the onboarding status dashboard, bottleneck/aging analysis, and outcome/benefits reporting capabilities.
- **Role/responsibility change:** Operations managers shift from reactive escalation-driven intervention to proactive, data-driven case management.
- **Policy/rule change:** KPI definitions, baselines, and targets must be formally approved and owned rather than informally tracked.

## 5. Change Impact Assessment
| Dimension | Current state | Future state | Gap / disruption |
|---|---|---|---|
| Process | Manual, periodic status compilation | Continuously available dashboard and scorecard | Requires trust in automated data over manually curated reports |
| Tools/systems | Spreadsheets, ad hoc reports | Onboarding dashboard, aging analysis, KPI scorecard | New reporting tool adoption across roles |
| Roles/skills | Reactive escalation management | Proactive, data-driven bottleneck management | Managers need to build habits around regular dashboard review |
| Volume/workload | Time spent assembling reports manually | Time redirected to acting on insights | Initial data-quality stabilization period expected |

## 7. Training and Enablement Needs
- Roles requiring formal training: operations managers and business leaders (dashboard interpretation), business analysts (KPI scorecard maintenance).
- Format: dashboard walkthrough session and quick-reference guide.
- Owner and target completion date: to confirm.
- Source material: UX / Interface Considerations sections of the three child feature canvases.

## 8. Resistance Risks and Mitigations
| Risk | Likely source | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Stakeholders distrust dashboard data over familiar manual reports | Business leaders, managers | Run the dashboard in parallel with manual reporting briefly and reconcile discrepancies |
| KPI baselines and targets remain unapproved | Product/business leadership | Escalate baseline and target approval as a go-live blocker (see source epic, Section 6) |
| Dashboard used for blame rather than improvement | Operations managers | Frame usage guidance around proactive support, not performance policing |
