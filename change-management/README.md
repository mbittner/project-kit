## How This Folder Is Organized
Change management documentation mirrors the epic/feature structure of the rest of the pack:

- **Executive summary** ([executive-summary.md](executive-summary.md)) — audience: executive sponsor, steering committee. A one-page, leadership-facing rollup of the change across all six epics: drivers, impacted stakeholders, top risks, readiness asks, and success measures.
- **Epic-level change briefs** (`cm-epic-XXX-*.md`) — audience: executive sponsor, business leadership, operations managers. Covers business driver, impacted stakeholder groups, communication plan, training needs, resistance risks, readiness criteria, and adoption measures for an entire epic.
- **Feature-level change briefs** (`cm-feat-XXX-*.md`) — audience: end users, trainers, front-line supervisors. Covers the before/after journey, job aids, local champion support, and adoption measures for a single feature.
- **Templates** ([cm-epic-template.md](../templates/cm-epic-template.md), [cm-feature-template.md](../templates/cm-feature-template.md)) — blank starting points for new briefs, kept in sync with the structure above.

## Suggested Reading Order
1. Start at the [parent initiative](../initiative/init-001-modernize-new-business-onboarding.md) for overall context and Measures of Success.
2. Read the epic-level change brief for the area you're responsible for (table above).
3. Follow its links down to the relevant feature-level change briefs once drafted, for user-facing training and adoption detail.
4. Use the templates ([cm-epic-template.md](../templates/cm-epic-template.md), [cm-feature-template.md](../templates/cm-feature-template.md)) to draft any missing brief, keeping the same section structure for consistency across the pack.

## Traceability
`Initiative Measures of Success → Epic change brief (adoption/benefits measurement) → Feature change brief (adoption measures) → Training/communication execution → Adoption evidence`
